About this prototype

The material classifier uses MobileNetV2 and recognizes six waste categories: cardboard, glass, metal, paper, plastic and trash.

Brand recognition is an experimental model currently limited to Coca-Cola family products and Predator bottles.

Predictions are decision-support results, not final verification of material composition, brand identity or environmental impact.
